How to play

  • Mix: tap a paint tube to squeeze paint into the glowing cup of the tray. Add a second color, then tap Stir or stir with a finger in circles over the tray. Each cup keeps its color. Tap a cup to light it and add more paint (orange + white = light orange). Wash cleans the glowing cup.
  • Paint: tap Paint at the bottom. The view turns to the easel and the cups line up under the paper. Tap a cup to dip the brush, then paint with a finger. Big and Small change the brush, Undo takes back the last stroke (and brings back the old sheet after New paper), Save keeps a picture on this device.
  • The poster on the wall always shows what to do next. Tap it to hear it.
  • The six cups are remembered on this device. The painting is not, so tap Save to keep it.

Why it happens

Primary colors of paint: red, yellow and blue. You cannot make them by mixing other paints. Secondary colors are made by mixing two primaries: red + yellow = orange, yellow + blue = green, red + blue = purple. Mixing all three makes brown. Complementary pairs such as red + green also make brown.

Tints and shades: adding white makes a lighter tint (red + white = pink, blue + white = light blue). Adding black makes a darker shade (dark red, dark blue).

Paint is different from light. Each paint soaks up (absorbs) some colors of light and bounces back the rest. When you mix paints, together they soak up more light, so mixes get darker and duller. Colored lights work the other way: they add up, and red + green light looks yellow. That is why we mix paint on a palette differently from lights on a wall.

The game uses the traditional red-yellow-blue (RYB) color wheel taught in preschool. Printers use cyan, magenta and yellow for cleaner mixes, but RYB is the classic starting point for children.

Ba màu cơ bản của màu vẽ là đỏ, vàng, xanh dương. Trộn hai màu cơ bản được màu thứ cấp: đỏ + vàng = cam, vàng + xanh dương = xanh lá, đỏ + xanh dương = tím. Trộn cả ba được màu nâu. Thêm trắng thì màu nhạt hơn (đỏ + trắng = hồng), thêm đen thì màu sẫm hơn. Màu vẽ hút bớt ánh sáng, nên càng trộn càng tối; còn ánh sáng màu thì cộng lại, nên pha ánh sáng khác với pha màu vẽ.

Questions to ask

  • Which two colors make green? Hai màu nào trộn thành xanh lá?
  • What happens if we add white? And black? Thêm màu trắng thì sao? Còn màu đen?
  • Can you make pink? How about brown? Con pha được màu hồng không? Còn màu nâu thì sao?
  • Which color do you want to paint the sun? Con muốn tô ông mặt trời màu gì?

Try it at home

Muffin-tin paint tray: put a spoon of washable paint (red, yellow, blue, white) in a few cups of an old muffin tin and leave the others empty. Your child mixes two colors in an empty cup with a craft stick, then paints with the new color. Use non-toxic, washable paint and stay nearby.

Khay pha màu bằng khuôn bánh: cho mỗi ô của khuôn bánh cũ một thìa màu vẽ (đỏ, vàng, xanh dương, trắng), để trống vài ô. Bé lấy que khuấy trộn hai màu trong ô trống rồi vẽ bằng màu mới. Dùng màu an toàn, rửa được và ba mẹ ở bên cạnh nhé.
